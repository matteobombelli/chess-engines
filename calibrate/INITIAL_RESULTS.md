# Initial Chess.com 30+0 calibration

Date: 2026-08-13

These are preliminary move-quality-equivalent estimates, not ratings earned by
playing full games on Chess.com.

## Corpus

- 1,000 unique rated, standard Chess.com games
- Exact `TimeControl` value `1800` (30+0); no other Rapid controls included
- Three starting users, followed by breadth-first opponent discovery
- 69 public player archives queried
- Global maximum of 20 corpus games for any one player
- Ratings in the corpus are concentrated between 400 and 1999; evidence above
  2000 is sparse
- One random eligible position per side per game, plies 12 through 60
- 1,851 candidate positions from 934 games
- 709 informative positions from 519 games after forced moves and essentially
  decided positions were excluded

The generated corpus is `data/chesscom-30-0.jsonl`. It is ignored by Git.

## Move grading

- Stockfish 17.1 with `UCI_ShowWDL`
- One thread, 128 MiB hash
- 20,000 nodes per search
- Hash cleared before every search
- Reference, historical human, and bot moves searched independently with the
  same node budget
- Loss is the reduction in expected game score relative to Stockfish's
  reference move
- Uncertainty resamples whole human players, preserving each person's repeated
  observations

## Results

| Bot configuration | Chess.com 30+0 equivalent | Uncertainty |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Random, seed 1 | below 400 | below the lowest populated calibration band |
| Minimax, fixed depth 3 | about 1,750 | approximately 1,550 to above 2,000 |

For Minimax, requiring at least 25 samples per 200-point band produced an
estimate of 1752, a player-bootstrap interval of 1560 through beyond the populated
1999-point boundary, and `R² = 0.846`. Allowing the smaller 2000–2199 band with
15 samples produced 1740 and an interval of 1565–2040 with `R² = 0.848`.

The generated analysis artifacts are `runs/random-30-0-initial.json` and
`runs/minimax-d3-30-0-initial.json`. They are ignored by Git.

## Interpretation

The Minimax point estimate is robust to the two tested band thresholds, but its
upper uncertainty remains broad because strong exact-30+0 games are scarce in
this corpus. A publishable result should use more independent seed users,
several thousand games, and the default 100,000 Stockfish nodes per search.

This benchmark measures move quality on positions generated by humans. It does
not measure clock management, resignation behavior, or whether the bot tends to
create easier or harder positions in complete games. Every reported rating must
therefore name the bot's search configuration; “Minimax” alone is not a fixed
playing strength.
